# Welcome to webette

Static sites from your folders. Simple, friendly, and ready to tweak.

webette is a filesystem‑first site builder for curious makers, artists, tinkerers, developers, and organizations.  
You put your content in folders, and webette weaves it into a working website.

**Everything is a Folder.**  
Collections are folders. Entries are subfolders. Blocks are just files.

## What webette generates

- clean HTML pages
- your assets (images, audio, video)
- an exported model in `_public/_model` for debugging and inspection
